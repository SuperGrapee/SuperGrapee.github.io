## Research Interests

- Flexible Electronics
- Eye tracking
- Artificial Intelligence

My current research focuses on practical problems that wearable and flexible electronic devices face in real life, especially in **Intelligent Healthcare**. My interests include the **Soft Electronics** and its applications in **Human-Computer Interfaces**. I am working on the next-generation wearable devices.
